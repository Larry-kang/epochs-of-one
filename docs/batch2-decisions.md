# Batch 2 — judgement calls (item e)

- **Odds table:** adds life expectancy at birth and under-5 mortality (per 1,000) from UN WPP 2024 (CC BY 3.0 IGO), taken at the midpoint year of the selected range. Both columns are sortable.
- **Future-era shocks:** none added. Future markets are already block-resampled from historical returns, which include the real crashes, so future lives see shocks at historical rates. Inventing named future events (wars, pandemics) would be speculation shown as if it were fact.
- **Tragic set (halts skip-to-end):** deaths (parent, grandparent, classmate, pet), serious injury or illness (illness, car accident, chronic illness, disability, depression, spouse in hospital), violent crime (robbery), disaster loss (flood, earthquake) and financial ruin (job loss, foreclosure, default, margin call, divorce). Minor setbacks such as a back injury, heat stroke or war news are left out.
- **CJK sans:** Noto Sans TC (SIL OFL 1.1), subset to the glyphs the app uses (~585 KB woff2) and served as "EO Sans TC" after Inter, so body text looks the same on every OS and works offline.
