# L2 conscription + L3 TW/US/JP/KR localisation (v0.11.0)

Batches L2 and L3 of the localisation plan. Approved decisions: conscription is in v1 (TW 1 year from 2024, KR about 18
months, with the historical term by era; US draft until 1973; JP none); jobs and institutions are localised per country;
elected roles stay rare transitions with no election storylines (unchanged from C0: `RARE_PATHS`, no new content).
Dates Asia/Taipei. Clean-room: written from the plan, the statutes and our own engine only.

## 1. Features

### L2 conscription (`src/engine/life/conscription.ts`)
- Applies to occupation lives (`occ`), own RNG stream `cs` (other streams and the legacy engine are unchanged).
- Service starts at the call-up age or, if later, at the end of full-time study (deferment), capped per country (TW 30, KR 28,
  US 25). The term is the row in force for the service year **and birth cohort** (TW's 4-month rule for born 1994–2004 and
  the 1-year rule for born 2005+ are cohort-based).
- Months in service earn conscript pay (推估 share of the mean wage; TW 0.45 from 2024 when pay rose to ≈ NT$26k/month,
  0.15 before; KR 0.05 → 0.15 (2017) → 0.40 (2024); US 0.30) instead of the wage; no job-loss draw while serving full-time.
- Alternative service: TW 替代役 from 2000 (12% 推估), KR 사회복무요원 (10% 推估), DE Zivildienst 1961–2011 — label/event only.
- KR athlete exemption hook: a pro `athlete` at service age is exempt with 8% probability (推估: medal-based exemption).
- Women are never called up where the law conscripts men only; Israel's row has `monthsF` = 24.
- Events (zh-TW + en; ko for KR): `cs_start_*`, `cs_alt_*`, `cs_end_*` (TWN/KOR/USA local text, `gen` elsewhere), `cs_exempt_athlete`.
  ID card: a 兵役 row while serving (with a 推估 chip when the row or share is estimated).
- Coverage: T1 era history for TW, KR, US, JP, DE, GB, FR, BR, MX, CN; T2 current-law flag + term for ISR, SGP, GRC, CYP, AUT,
  FIN, CHE, TUR, RUS, VNM, THA, EGY, IRN. Every other country: off (T3 default).
- **Not modelled (decision needed, §6):** conflict mortality for draftees (e.g. US Korea/Vietnam, KR 1950s), pre-1945 imperial
  conscription (JP, colonial TW/KR), TW pre-1951, reserve call-ups.

### L3 TW / US / JP / KR (`src/engine/life/institutions.ts`, `src/content/l3-local.json`)
- **Institutions by era** (`public/data/l2/institutions.json`): school system, high-school and university entrance exams,
  civil-service exam, health insurance, pension scheme, labour-market note — each row with its start year, zh-TW + en, ja for
  JP and ko for KR. Examples: TW 聯考 → 學測/指考 (2002) → 學測/分科 (2022), 基測 (2001) → 會考 (2014), 勞保 → NHI (1995),
  新制勞退 (2005); US SAT/ACT, Medicare (1965), ACA (2014), 401(k) (1980); JP 共通一次 (1979) → センター (1990) → 共通テスト (2021),
  国民皆保険・皆年金 (1961); KR 예비고사 → 학력고사 (1982) → 수능 (1994), 의료보험 (1977) → universal (1989) → NHIS (2000), 국민연금 (1988).
- **Events** (stream `inst`, narrative): high-school exam at 15; university exam at 18 with a retake chance (重考 / 浪人 / 재수;
  probability from the smart stat and the study focus, 推估); civil-service exam attempts when entering `civil_servant` /
  `judge_prosecutor` in TW/JP/KR (1–3 attempts, 推估); scheme launch events (NHI 1995 etc.) for adults; US Medicare at 65.
- **Mechanics (all 推估, small):**
  - US job-tied insurance: each unemployment spell before 65 costs 3% (pre-1986) / 4% (COBRA era) / 1.5% (ACA, 2014+) of the
    mean income in premiums and bills (cash, not income).
  - JP 正社員 / 非正規: private employees draw a status at entry with the era's non-regular share (12% 1950 → 37.5% 2015+,
    shaped on the Labour Force Survey trend, not ingested → 推估); non-regular wage 0.7 × R and job-loss ×1.5, regular R and
    ×0.75; 3%/yr conversion to regular. R normalises on the career-average non-regular share, so mean wages are unchanged.
    The ID card shows a 正社員/非正規 badge.
  - KR 명예퇴직: from 1998, white-collar large-firm occupations aged 50–56 take honourable early retirement with 4%/yr
    probability → `food_retail_su` (self-employment) with 1 × mean income severance.
- **Local occupation names:** ja and ko names for all 80 occupations (`names`), and country-local equivalents (`local`) for
  TW (高普考 civil servant, 縣市議員, 立法委員, 志願役士官兵 …), US (Government employee, City council member, Member of
  Congress, Governor / cabinet secretary, Draftee …), JP (公務員（国家・地方）, 自衛官, 国会議員, 宅建士 …) and KR (공무원 9급·7급,
  지방의원, 국회의원, 부사관, 프로게이머 …). `occName(id, lang, iso)`; vi falls back to English.
- **Elected roles:** unchanged rare transitions (local_rep → legislator → minister); only their local names were added.
  No election storylines.
- **Saves:** a pre-v0.11.0 save gets `l2From` = years played (`migrateSaveCfgL2`), so replayed years are byte-identical and
  conscription/institutions only apply from the resume age. New lives carry `l2: true`.

Findings:
- **Conscription costs men, by law, and only men** (women's csEffect = 1.000 everywhere except Israel's row). Lifetime cost:
  TW −1.9% (born 1950, 27 m) → −0.3% (born 2005, 12 m at the higher 2024 pay); KR −1.4/−1.5% (born 1970/1990; −12% in the
  born-1950 cohort, 36 m with a token allowance); US −0.04% (born 1950, 5% drafted); JP 0.
- **Early-career effect is larger:** TW men lose 12–15% of income in their first 10 working years (born 1950/1970), 6% (born
  1990), 2% (born 2005); KR 12–16% (born 1970/1990), 5% (born 2005). Careers are delayed, not capped: the ladder and transitions
  are unchanged; experience keeps accruing during service (a simplification, see §6).
- Because the legacy engine has no conscription, men's on/off ratio falls and women's F/M rises with conscription (TW born
  1970: F/M 1.080 → 1.092; KR born 1990: 1.076 → 1.093). This is a real, law-based effect, documented rather than corrected.
- Every TW/US/JP country × sex × cohort cell stays inside ±8% of the pre-occupation engine except: KOR born 1970 women 0.889
  and men 0.930 (pre-existing KOR 1970 gap, §3), KOR born 1950 (legacy engine anomaly: F/M off = 7.8 — see §6) (JPN cells were rerun after the JP normalisation fix: 0.948–1.021).
- L3 hooks are sex-neutral by construction (no sex read anywhere in `institutions.ts`, static test); their effects are equal
  for men and women (instEffect M vs F within 0.4 pts in every cell).
- JP non-regular work is strongly female-skewed in reality; v0.11 models it sex-neutral because no ILOSTAT sex series for it is
  shipped (equity policy: sex only where ILOSTAT data exists). Flagged for L7.
- Protected attributes (ethnicity, religion, caste, region, names) enter nothing.

## 6. Decisions (owner, 2026-10-17: all approved as recommended)
1. **KOR born 1970 = 0.915 vs the pre-occupation engine: accepted as the conscription effect** (v0.10 was 0.923; about
   30 months of service for men). A new guardrail measures KOR/TWN/USA against "occupations without conscription" (§3a).
2. Wartime death risk for conscripts → L7-2 (`docs/l7-tracking.md`).
3. Conscription before 1945 / 1951 → L7-3.
4. **Work experience keeps accruing during service: accepted** (simplification; careers are delayed in income, not in the
   experience curve).
5. **Exams are narrative only: accepted** (they never gate entry; pass rates stay 推估 until official data is ingested).
6. Retirement age and pension type by era → the calibration pass after L4.
7. Gazette citations for non-L3 conscription rows → L4–L6.
8. JP non-regular employment by sex → L7-4.
Also noted: KOR born 1950 legacy F/M anomaly (7.8, pre-existing) for L7.
